03 // STRUCTURAL RIGOR & FOUNDATIONS 構造耐力

WHAT YOU DON’T SEE
MAKES IT STAND FOREVER.

Finishing is easy; structural perfection is rare. At R.S. Constructions, our obsession starts 15 feet below the topsoil with deep foundation footings, continuous grade-beam tying, and dense rebar steel cage detailing.

01

Fe550D Grade Steel Exclusively

We mandate high-ductility TMT steel bars (TATA Tiscon / SAIL / JSW) with certified tensile testing for supreme earthquake flexibility.

02

Machine-Vibrated Concrete Compaction

Zero honeycombing. Every column and slab pour is compacted with needle vibrators to achieve 100% density and impermeable strength.

03

14-Milestone Quality Sign-Offs

From soil bearing capacity tests to 7, 14, and 28-day concrete cube compressive tests, every milestone is signed off by our directors.
